##  INTRESTS

### MECHANICAL DESIGN AND CAD

Interested in 3D modelling, mechanical component design, mechanism development, product design, and design for manufacturing. I want to understand not only how to model a component but also how to design it for functionality, reliability, and practical implementation.

### ENGINEERING SIMULATIONS AND ANALYSIS

Interested in using engineering analysis and simulation to understand the behaviour of mechanical systems. My areas of interest include **Finite Element Analysis (FEA), structural analysis, and simulation-driven design**, with the goal of connecting theoretical calculations with computational results.

### ROBOTICS

Interested in robotic mechanisms, automation, actuators, and electromechanical systems. I am particularly interested in the mechanical side of robotics, including mechanism design, component integration, and the interaction between mechanical hardware, electronics, and control systems.

### CORE MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Building my understanding of fundamental disciplines such as solid mechanics, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, heat transfer, and manufacturing engineering. I believe these fundamentals are essential for making sound engineering decisions and designing reliable systems.
